Hero Word Guide

Quick Review before or during your mission!

🚦 1. Rules, Laws & Community

  • • Rule (Kural): Instructions at school or home. (Example: Listen to your teacher.)
  • • Law (Yasa / Kanun): Official rules for the whole country. (Example: Stop at a red traffic light.)
  • • Obey / Follow the rules (Kurallara uymak): Doing what the rules say. ✅
  • • Break the rules (Kuralları çiğnemek): NOT following the rules. ❌
  • • Community (Toplum / Çevre): All the people living in one place together.
  • • Good citizen (İyi vatandaş): A person who helps the community and obeys laws.

⚖️ 2. Rights vs. Responsibilities

  • • Right (Hak): Something every person deserves to have!
    → Human right, Children's right, Student's right (Going to school, playing safely, having clean water).
  • • Responsibility (Sorumluluk / Görev): Your duty; something you MUST do.
    → Responsible for (-den sorumlu): "I am responsible for doing my homework and feeding my cat."

🤝 3. Respect & Politeness

  • • Polite (Kibar / Nazik) ✅: Saying "Please", "Thank you", and "Excuse me".
  • • Impolite = Rude (Kaba) ❌: Shouting at people, pushing in line, not saying thank you.
  • • Respect (Saygı / Saygı duymak): Being kind and listening to others.

⚡ 4. Word Power: Suffix (-ful) & Negative Prefixes (im-, un-, dis-)

➕ Suffix "-ful" = FULL OF (Positive!)

Added to the END of a word:

  • • Respect + ful = Respectful (Full of respect)
  • • Help + ful = Helpful (Full of help)
  • • Care + ful = Careful (Full of care)

➖ Prefixes "im- / un- / dis-" = NOT (Negative!)

Added to the START of a word:

  • • im + polite = Impolite (NOT polite / rude)
  • • dis + obey = Disobey (NOT obey)
  • • dis + respectful = Disrespectful (NOT respectful)
  • • un + fair / kind = Unfair / Unkind
