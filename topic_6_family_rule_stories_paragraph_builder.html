Connector Guide: both, but, because

Learn how to link your ideas in a paragraph!

1. BOTH (İkisi de)

Use both to show similarities between parents and grandparents.

✨ "Both my parents and grandparents want me to study hard."

2. BUT (Fakat / Ama)

Use but to show differences (contrast) between generations.

✨ "My parents are strict about bedtime, but my grandparents let me stay up late."

3. BECAUSE (Çünkü)

Use because to give the reason behind a rule.

✨ "I must go to bed early because I need to wake up for school."
