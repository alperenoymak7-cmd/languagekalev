Comparative Adverbs Guide

1. The Main Rule (more + adverb + than):

To compare HOW two people do an action with standard -ly adverbs, use:

more + adverb + than

Examples: more quickly than, more silently than, more loudly than, more clearly than.

2. Important Exceptions (Short / Irregular):

Some short adverbs do not use more:

fast → faster than | badly → worse than
