Past Obligations Helper

1. Past Obligation (Must in the past):

Use HAD TO + Base Verb (V1) for rules/duties in the past.

Example: Yesterday, I had to clean my bedroom.

2. No Past Obligation (Not necessary):

Use DIDN'T HAVE TO + Base Verb (V1)

Example: On Sunday, I didn't have to wake up early.

3. Golden Rule:

Never say "didn't had to" or "hadn't to". Always use didn't have to + V1!
