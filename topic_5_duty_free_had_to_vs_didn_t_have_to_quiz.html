Duty Guide: Had to vs. Didn't have to

Learn past obligations before or during the game!

📜

HAD TO = PAST OBLIGATION

(Geçmişte yapmak zorundaydım)

Use had to when you had no choice and it was compulsory in the past! (Works for ALL subjects: I, you, he, she, we, they).

📜 "Yesterday, I had to finish my homework."

➔ It was compulsory!

🏖️

DIDN'T HAVE TO = NO OBLIGATION

(Geçmişte yapmak zorunda değildim)

Use didn't have to when there was no need or compulsion—you were totally free to choose!

🏖️ "It was Sunday, so I didn't have to wake up early."

➔ No need / Free choice!

✨ Golden Rule: Clean Base Verb (V1) after had to / didn't have to!

Formula: had to / didn't have to + V1 (I, you, he, she, we, they — NO -s, NO to, NO ing!)

✅ She had to go to the doctor.
✅ We didn't have to wear uniforms.
❌ She had to went.
❌ We didn't have to wearing.
