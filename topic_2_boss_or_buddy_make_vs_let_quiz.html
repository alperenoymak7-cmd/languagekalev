Grammar Power Guide: Make vs. Let

Learn the secret rules before or during the game!

😤

MAKE = FORCE

(Zorlamak / Mecbur tutmak)

You do NOT have a choice. Someone forces you to do it!

🥦 "My mom makes me eat vegetables."

➔ I don't want to, but I have to!

😊

LET = PERMISSION

(İzin vermek)

You WANT to do it, and someone says: "Yes, you can!"

🍦 "My mom lets me eat ice cream."

➔ I want to, and she says YES!

🌟 Golden Rule #1: NO "to" and NO "-ing" after the person!

Formula: make / let + person (me, you, us, him, her) + V1 (clean verb)

✅ My dad makes me study.
✅ She lets me play tablet.
❌ My dad makes me to study.
❌ She lets me playing tablet.

⚡ Golden Rule #2: Present Simple Rules (-s, don't/doesn't, do/does)

Subject (Who?) Positive (+) Negative (-) Question (?)
I / You / We / They
(My parents, Teachers) 		make
let 		don't make
don't let 		Do they make...?
Do your parents let...?
He / She / It
(My mom, My dad, The teacher) 		makes
lets 		doesn't make
doesn't let 		Does she make...?
Does your mom let...?

⚠️ Watch Out! After doesn't or Does, the verb loses its -s! (✅ doesn't let — ❌ doesn't lets)
