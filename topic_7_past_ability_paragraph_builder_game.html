Quick Grammar Helper

1. Past Ability (Positive):

Use COULD + Base Verb (V1)

Example: When I was 5, I could run fast.

2. Past Inability (Negative):

Use COULDN'T + Base Verb (V1)

Example: I couldn't swim in deep water.

3. Linking Words for Paragraphs:
  • BUT: Connects opposites (could ride bike, but couldn't drive).
  • BECAUSE: Gives reasons (couldn't read because I was 3).
