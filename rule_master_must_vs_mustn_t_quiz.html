Rule Master Guide: Must vs. Mustn't

Learn the rules before or during the game!

✅

MUST = OBLIGATION

(Zorunluluk / Yapmalısın)

Use must when something is necessary, important, or required by a rule!

✅ "I must study for my English exam."

➔ It is necessary!

❌

MUSTN'T = PROHIBITION

(Yasak / Yapmamalısın)

Use mustn't when something is forbidden or against the rules!

❌ "You mustn't run in the school hallway."

➔ It is forbidden / dangerous!

🌟 Golden Rule: Clean Base Verb (V1)!

Formula: must / mustn't + V1 (clean verb for ALL subjects: I, you, he, she, we, they)

✅ She must go home.
✅ They mustn't touch this.
❌ She must goes home.
❌ They mustn't to touch this.
