Time Traveler Guide: Could vs. Couldn't

Learn past abilities before or during the game!

🌟

COULD = PAST ABILITY

(Geçmişte yapabilmek)

Use could when you had the skill or ability to do something in the past!

🌟 "When I was 5, I could ride a bicycle."

➔ I had the ability!

🚫

COULDN'T = NO PAST ABILITY

(Geçmişte yapamamak)

Use couldn't (could not) when you did not have the skill in the past!

🚫 "When I was a baby, I couldn't speak English."

➔ I did not have the ability!

✨ Golden Rule: Clean Base Verb (V1) for ALL subjects!

Formula: could / couldn't + V1 (I, you, he, she, we, they — NO -s, NO to, NO ing!)

✅ She could swim when she was 6.
✅ They couldn't read at age 2.
❌ She could swims.
❌ They couldn't to read.
